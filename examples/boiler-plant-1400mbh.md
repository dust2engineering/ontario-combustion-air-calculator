# Worked example 2 — boiler plant, 1,400 MBH, all draft control

Over 400 MBH, Clause 8.4.2 applies:
1,000,000 / 7,000 + 400,000 / 14,000 = 142.86 + 28.57 = **171.43 in²** supply
opening (6–18 in above floor), plus the 8.4.1 ventilation opening:
10% of 171.43 = **17.14 in²** at the highest practicable point.
